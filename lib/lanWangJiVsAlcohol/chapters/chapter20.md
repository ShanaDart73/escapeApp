<br />

Embarrassed, Jīn Líng said angrily, “That’s because Jīn Chǎn was hiding in that kind of place. Do you think I wanted to go there?”

Lán SīZhuī hurriedly tried to smooth things over, saying, “Of course not.”

Lán JǐngYí struck again, asking, “Then what about you getting kidnapped?”

Jīn Líng’s expression darkened. Gritting his teeth, he said, “Please, just finish what you were saying – tell me what else Wèi WúXiàn said.”

Lán JǐngYí replied, “Oh yes, he also said that after you had managed to escape from your kidnappers, you got scared by Xiǎo-Píngguǒ and screamed so loudly that Xiǎo-Píngguǒ got frightened too and began to bray.”

Then, in disbelief, he added, “I mean... were you really scared by Xiǎo-Píngguǒ? Ouch! Why did you hit me? SīZhuī, help me!”

Speechless, Lán SīZhuī just watched as the two of them ran around the table, one chasing the other. Yet he couldn’t help but smile faintly at the scene unfolding before him.

The autumnal chill seeped quietly in through the open window, where the two lovers slept.

As was his usual routine, Lán WàngJī woke up early the next morning. He got out of bed and went to gently close the window, then returned to bed. His gaze rested tenderly upon Wèi WúXiàn, who was sleeping soundly.

Then, frowning with concern, Lán WàngJī thought about how busy Wèi WúXiàn had been since the day before yesterday. They had a long journey before reaching Lánlíng City. As soon as they arrived, Wèi WúXiàn rushed to rescue Jīn Líng, and then the three of them went to the brothel to capture Jīn Chǎn. For the sake of training Jīn Líng, Wèi WúXiàn spent the whole night supervising him while he was fighting.

He continued thinking, “He didn’t get a proper rest all this time and looks so exhausted. This is also partly my fault.”

Yesterday morning, after leaving the brothel and ensuring that Jīn Líng had returned safely, Lán WàngJī and Wèi WúXiàn went to the inn. By the time they finished interrogating Jīn Chǎn, it was nearly evening. Lán WàngJī had earlier instructed the innkeeper to prepare hot water and food.

Once Wèi WúXiàn was done with Jīn Chǎn, Lán WàngJī assigned Lán SīZhuī and Lán JǐngYí to escort Jīn Chǎn to Jīnlíntái. Then he took the weary-looking Wèi WúXiàn in his arms and carried him to the room.

Wèi WúXiàn chuckled and said, “HánGuāng-Jūn, have you become addicted to hugging me? We were in public, after all.”

Lán WàngJī’s expression remained unchanged as he placed Wèi WúXiàn on the bed, then replied, “There’s no need to be concerned about that.”

He then asked, “Didn’t you say that wherever you are with your beloved, you don’t need to be self-conscious?”

Wèi WúXiàn thought for a brief instant, then exclaimed in surprise, “Lán Zhàn, do you... remember what I said despite being drunk?”

Lán WàngJī’s ears turned red. He nodded slightly, then looked into Wèi WúXiàn’s eyes and said, “I remember everything you told me.”

Wèi WúXiàn knew that Lán WàngJī was referring to what he had told him when they were in the kitchen at Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù.

Smiling, he said, “That’s great! Usually, when you get drunk, you don’t remember anything after you sober up. The demonic wine is actually quite—”

Wèi WúXiàn suddenly interrupted himself, as though something had crossed his mind.

Lán WàngJī didn’t say anything. He just sat down beside him and put his arm around him.

Shaking his head, Wèi WúXiàn said, “Lán Zhàn, don’t. I reek of perfume right now. I want to have a bath.”

The innkeeper had already filled the bathtub with warm water and brought soap and other necessities into the room.

Wèi WúXiàn hurried over, quickly took off his clothes and got into the bath.

Lán WàngJī quietly picked up the clothes Wèi WúXiàn had scattered on the floor. The scent of cosmetics on them made him frown. He set them aside to be taken to the laundry room once Wèi WúXiàn had finished bathing. He then placed a set of clean clothes on the table.

After a while, the sound of the bathwater could no longer be heard – an utter silence had fallen over the room.

Puzzled, Lán WàngJī looked towards the bath and saw only soap bubbles rising above the water.

He quickly walked over and found Wèi WúXiàn fast asleep. He then took care of everything, from washing him and drying him off to dressing him in his undergarments and putting him in bed. Afterwards, he too had a bath.

Shortly after he had finished, Lán WàngJī heard a gentle knock on the door. It was the innkeeper bringing their supper.

As though he had sensed that Lán WàngJī was not lying beside him, Wèi WúXiàn, half-asleep, stretched out his arm, searching for him.

Lán WàngJī immediately took his hand, leaned over and kissed him on the forehead, then said, “Wèi Yīng, eat something before going back to sleep.”

Wèi WúXiàn opened his sleepy eyes slightly and saw Lán WàngJī sitting beside him. He suddenly began, “Lán Zhàn...”

As he caressed Wèi WúXiàn’s cheek, Lán WàngJī replied tenderly, “I’m right here.”

“...the wine was brewed using the usual method,” Wèi WúXiàn continued. “Sì-Shū* taught me how to make it. When I tried, I couldn’t get it right.”

<small>*Sì-Shū literally means fourth uncle. He is one of the Wen Clan members Wèi WúXiàn rescued.</small>

At the mention of Sì-Shū, Lán WàngJī couldn’t help but think of the Burial Mounds, where not a single blade of grass could grow and the bones of corpses lay scattered across the ground.

Feeling his eyes sting, Lán WàngJī clenched his fist. He didn’t know what to say.

Wèi WúXiàn went on, “It was so troublesome that I scribbled down, ‘Why not cultivate a demon to brew the wine for me?’ in my manuscript.”

He added, “I wanted to drink alcohol to forget everything. But in the end, it was fruitless...”

Lán WàngJī listened quietly until Wèi WúXiàn fell asleep.

He then thought, “Back then, in the dark and cold nights of the Burial Mounds, what were you thinking of? When you had neither wine to drink nor friends to talk to, did you ever regret your decision? Had you ever thought of me?”

It was not until the sun grew brighter and the noise outside became louder that Wèi WúXiàn woke up. Yawning, he got out of bed and asked, “Lán Zhàn, what time is it?”

Lán WàngJī walked over and handed him some clean clothes, then replied, “It’s just past nine. Do you want to sleep a bit longer?”

Wèi WúXiàn rubbed his empty stomach and replied with a grimace, “No need. I woke up feeling hungry. Besides, I went to bed early last night, so I’ve had plenty of sleep.”

He then glanced at the table and, as expected, saw a variety of breakfast dishes that had already been laid out. Their aroma made his mouth water.

Overjoyed, Wèi WúXiàn hugged Lán WàngJī and gave him a kiss. He quickly got dressed and went to wash his face. Then, without combing his hair, he sat at the table and started eating breakfast with a hearty appetite.

Lán WàngJī took a wooden comb, then sat behind Wèi WúXiàn. He gently began to run the comb from the roots to the ends of his hair, smoothing it out as he went.

The sensation of the comb running through his hair made him feel so good that he slowed down his eating. He chuckled and began to recite the Hair-Combing Blessings.

<em>“First comb, all the way to the ends: honour one another with mutual respect.”</em><br />
<em>“Second comb, all the way to the ends: may you both live together as one.”</em>

After he finished combing Wèi WúXiàn’s hair, Lán WàngJī neatly tied it into a knot using Wèi WúXiàn’s red ribbon. Then, with deep affection, he embraced him from behind, pressed a kiss to the nape of his neck, and recited the third blessing.

<em>“Third comb, all the way to the ends: you shall never be parted until your hair turns white.”</em>

Wèi WúXiàn couldn’t help repeating the third blessing, “Let’s grow old together and never be parted.”

He slowly ran his fingers along Lán WàngJī’s arm until they reached his hand. Then he intertwined his fingers with his. Smiling, he looked at him and said, “Lán Zhàn, let’s go to Yúnmèng.”

Lán WàngJī agreed, of course. He would never refuse him.

Instead of flying upon Bìchén, they boarded a boat and sailed downstream, amid the scenery of the stars in the sky and the green mountains on either side of the river. The evening breeze caressed them along the way.

Free from worldly cares, with only the gentle tune of the guqin to keep them company, they listened as though they alone could truly understand its notes. Their hearts at peace, they let the music flow with serene simplicity, transcending the boundaries of past and present.

Drifting leisurely, the boat gradually transitioned from the stillness of the mountains to the bustling crowds of Yúnmèng.

Wèi WúXiàn stepped onto the dock, where the disciples of YúnmèngJiāng had been stationed to ensure the safety of incoming and outgoing vessels and passengers.

When they noticed Wèi WúXiàn and Lán WàngJī, their eyes lit up. One of them stepped forward and performed the fist-and-hand salute, saying, “Wèi-gōngzǐ, Jiāng Zōng-zhǔ instructed us to wait here for your arrival. Please do follow me.”

Surprised, Wèi WúXiàn raised an eyebrow and asked, “Did Jiāng Chéng know that I was coming?”

The disciple replied, “He simply said that if we saw you, we should bring you to the Lotus Piers. If you didn’t show up, he would go and see you.”

Wèi WúXiàn thought, “Could it be that Jiāng Chéng has figured out the purpose of my coming to Yúnmèng?”

He turned to face Lán WàngJī, took his hand and said, “Lán Zhàn, I came here to retrieve something from Jiāng Chéng, and it seems like he has anticipated my intentions.”

Lán WàngJī pondered for a brief moment, then asked, “Do you want me to go with you?”

Wèi WúXiàn ran a finger along Lán WàngJī’s palm and, with an air of mystery, said, “No. If you come with me, there won’t be any surprise for you.”

Lán WàngJī was moved. He hadn’t expected that the reason for their long journey to Yúnmèng was that Wèi WúXiàn had a surprise for him.

He then asked, “Where do you want me to wait for you?”

“Wherever it suits you,” Wèi WúXiàn replied with a smile. “I’ll find you. Trust me.”

He then added, “Since you’re here, why don’t you have a look around the city?”

After four years, this was the first time Wèi WúXiàn had entered the Lotus Piers residence through the main entrance.

When Jiāng Chéng saw Wèi WúXiàn being escorted, he gestured for the disciple to leave. Then, unlike him, he didn’t say much. He simply walked ahead to lead the way.

Whenever they passed a building that hadn’t existed when they were teenagers, Jiāng Chéng would stop and explain what the building was used for.

Wèi WúXiàn followed Jiāng Chéng in silence, glancing at the new buildings that had been erected over the past ten years or so, after the residence had been burned down – a painful memory that had stayed with him all these years.

When they reached the ancestral hall, Jiāng Chéng waited outside as Wèi WúXiàn entered the hall after solemnly straightening his clothes.

He walked over to the memorial tablets, lit the incense, and knelt down to pay his respects. Then he looked at the names engraved on the tablets, one by one, and began to recall events from the past: how Lady Yu used to punish him by making him kneel there, while Jiāng Chéng waited for him behind the door, and when he rested his head on Jiāng YànLí’s lap and innocently asked her why people fell in love.

When his gaze fell upon Jiāng YànLí’s memorial tablet, tears quietly rolled down his cheeks.

His eyes curved into crescents as he smiled, then whispered, “Shījiě, last time when I came here, I brought someone with me. That person is the one I want to spend the rest of my life with. He’s truly wonderful. I’m deeply in love with him.”

Jiāng Chéng had been silently staring at his back. He then watched as Wèi WúXiàn slowly stood up and walked to the door, concealing his grief beneath an air of nonchalance.

Shortly after they had left the ancestral hall, Jiāng Chéng suddenly asked, “Where is Lán WàngJī?”

Leaning against the railing, Wèi WúXiàn remarked, “I thought you had decided not to mention him.”

Jiāng Chéng shot him a sidelong glance. Then, resisting the urge to criticise his posture, he handed a sword case to Wèi WúXiàn, saying irritably, “Here’s your sword.”

Keeping his cool, Wèi WúXiàn took the case and gently opened it. Inside lay his sword, Suíbiàn. He slowly ran his fingers along the carved scabbard until they touched the hilt. Then he gripped the sword and unsheathed it with a clang.

Jiāng Chéng’s heart skipped a beat as he watched Wèi WúXiàn’s red spiritual energy swirl around the sword. He clenched his fists in excitement. He was overjoyed to see that sight once more after so many years.

Nostalgically, Wèi WúXiàn slid his hand along the flat side of the sword, savouring its familiar feel. Then, as he used to when showing off his swordsmanship, he performed an elaborate display of swordplay. Once he had finished, he sheathed Suíbiàn and secured it to his waistband.

“It feels a bit strange,” Wèi WúXiàn commented as he adjusted his sword. “By the way, Jiāng Chéng, how did you know that I’d come to retrieve Suíbiàn?”

Seeing Wèi WúXiàn wearing a sword, Jiāng Chéng was so mesmerised that he stared at him for a while before replying, “At Jīnlíntái, I saw Jīn Líng use one of your sword techniques.”

He continued, “With your meridians locked, you wouldn’t be able to unleash your spiritual energy to use a sword. Since you did, then, somehow, you must have managed to unlock your meridians.”

Wèi WúXiàn’s interest was piqued. He asked, “Which sword technique did he use?”

Jiāng Chéng snorted, then replied, “The one you used to slice a watermelon into four pieces in one move.”

Wèi WúXiàn froze for a moment, then the corner of his mouth twitched. Unable to hold back, he slid down along the railing as he burst into laughter.

Jiāng Chéng immediately exploded, saying, “Of all the things you could teach, why did you have to teach him that rubbish?”

He went on, “Don’t just lie there laughing. Get up!”

Extremely amused, Wèi WúXiàn retorted, “It’s because you’re so overly protective that you refuse to spar with him. If you don’t give him any pointers, he won’t be able to improve his footwork.”

Jiāng Chéng could only grit his teeth, then awkwardly said, “Well, many thanks.”

Wèi WúXiàn knew straight away that Jiāng Chéng had thanked him for rescuing Jīn Líng, not for teaching him sword techniques.

He glanced up at the sky for a brief instant, then decided that it was time to get going. He stood up and, with a smile, said, “Jiāng Chéng, I’m off.”

Jiāng Chéng was dazed for a moment, then, with a stern expression, turned his head away and said, “If you want to leave, then just leave.”

He then added, “Don’t you usually leave without saying anything?”

Wèi WúXiàn didn’t reply. Instead, he cast a sword incantation and leapt into the air. Suíbiàn immediately unsheathed itself and came to rest flat beneath his feet. He then waved at Jiāng Chéng.

Wèi WúXiàn’s eyes were brimming with the joy at finally regaining possession of his sword. Unable to wait any longer, he flew into the sky.

Turning his head, he saw Jiāng Chéng, who couldn’t resist walking a few steps towards him, staring up at him.

Smiling, Wèi WúXiàn waved energetically at him, and then, like a swan gliding across the sky, he headed towards the one he cherished.

Jiāng Chéng knew that Wèi WúXiàn’s departure this time was not the final farewell but a promise they would meet again.

Although separated by mountains and rivers, from then on they would share the same sky and look upon each other as family.

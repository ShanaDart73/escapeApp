Although the same river flowed through both locations, there was a striking difference in their landscapes. Unlike the misty drizzle and the poetic charm of Gūsū’s small bridges and streams, Yúnmèng was a vivid picture of the hustle and bustle of everyday life.

“It’s true that the land shapes the people living in it,” Lán WàngJī thought as he strolled through the streets.

Despite the passage of many years and several changes, the city remained as lively as ever. Shops of all kinds lined the streets, with people coming and going, chatting and socialising from time to time. The locals sitting in small groups outside their doors warmly invited passers-by to join them.

With his sleeves gently fluttering as he walked, Lán WàngJī exuded a cool, serene aura, his fair skin and graceful demeanour making him stand out clearly from all the bustle around him.

He didn’t come to Yúnmèng often; if he did, it was only because of Wèi WúXiàn. He still remembered how the cheerful young boy had invited him over with a face full of hope. Still, he had never accepted his invitation.

However, unable to resist, Lán WàngJī would come to Yúnmèng just to taste the lotus pods that Wèi WúXiàn had praised so highly and see whether they were as delicious as he had claimed.

For a moment, it felt as though he had returned to the past. People around him were sizing him up just as they had back then, when he had travelled thousands of miles simply to catch a glimpse of the young boy cultivating demonic power and ascertain that he was safe and sound.

At that precise moment, the scent of wine wafting through the air reached his nose. Instinctively, he looked up at the first floor of a nearby tavern, expecting to see a young boy sitting on the balcony, his long black hair blowing in the gentle breeze, holding a jar of wine and smiling at him.

The mere sight of Wèi WúXiàn at that moment was so breathtaking that his long journey to Yúnmèng had been worthwhile.

He then stopped in his tracks, as though he had been engulfed in a mist that suddenly dissipated, waking him from a dream in which the past had overlapped with the present.

He whispered, “Wèi Yīng, I miss you.”

Cutting through the teeming city and transcending the passage of time, the melody of WàngXiàn drifted through the air like cherry blossom petals. It seemed as though Lán WàngJī’s whisper had been heard.

Amid the gasps of the crowd, Lán WàngJī soared into the sky upon his sword, his robes whipping in the wind, completely captivated by the notes of the flute.

As he flew over a mountain peak, the sound of the flute suddenly stopped. His heart pounding, he landed in a landscape covered in deep-red autumn foliage.

Lán WàngJī had no time to admire the scenery. Impatiently, he began to look around for Wèi WúXiàn. As he was about to call out, the leaves on the ground were suddenly swept into the air by a surge of sword energy.

Like a tapestry of love, the maple leaves surrounded Lán WàngJī layer by layer. Then he sensed another stir of the breeze behind him. As he turned around, he was instantly rendered speechless.

Holding Suíbiàn in one hand and a jar of wine in the other, Wèi WúXiàn was standing a few steps away, smiling at him.

He drank the wine and then flung the jar behind before running to Lán WàngJī and throwing himself into his arms. The young boy, whose swordplay had astonished the world, had finally returned to his side.

From their very first unforgettable meeting to the blossoming of their love, they would hold each other just as they did at that moment, with all the tenderness in the world, and never be parted until death.

The end.

<b>Extra 2:

Lán Qǐrén Is Having A Bad Day</b>

Having finished giving the lesson on the four key periods of the GūsūLán ancestors, Lán Qǐrén returned to his room for his customary meditation. Just then, Lán XīChén came to see him.

After performing a hand-and-fist salute, Lán XīChén smiled and reported, “Uncle, our physicians have just informed me that the people whom WàngJī and WúXiàn rescued during their Night Hunting have regained consciousness and are in excellent health. I intend to have our disciples escort them back to Lánlíng.”

Lán Qǐrén nodded, then seemed to remember something. He frowned and asked, “How is WàngJī? Is he still under the influence of alcohol?”

As he thought of his younger brother, Lán XīChén reflected on how difficult it was to be an elder brother. Not only had Lán WàngJī’s condition failed to improve, but he had also come to blows with Jiāng Chéng.

He replied, “He hasn’t sobered up yet. WúXiàn is looking after him. He should be resting as we speak.”

Lán Qǐrén was about to ask why he was still resting at that hour, but upon remembering his nephew’s unusual behaviour earlier, he frowned ever more deeply and decided against it.

He thought that Lán WàngJī should stay in his room for the time being so as to prevent him from behaving improperly in public and damaging his reputation as a role model.

The following day, Lán Qǐrén made his way to the Orchid Room to carry out a spot check on the previous day’s lessons, as was his usual practice.

As he approached the classroom, he heard the disciples engaged in a lively discussion. Lán Qǐrén, who always preferred peace and quiet, thought, “What kind of behaviour is this?”

As he pushed open the door, he heard one of them excitedly say, “HánGuāng-Jūn is so kind to Wèi-qiánbèi. Yesterday, after class, we saw him carrying Wèi-qiánbèi in his arms.”

They were all the disciples from other sects.

Lán Qǐrén froze in shock outside the door. “Wasn’t he supposed to be resting?” he thought.

Another disciple exclaimed, “There’s no way that HánGuāng-Jūn would do that!”

“It’s true,” another boy said. “There were at least twenty of us, and we saw it with our own eyes.”

Lán Qǐrén’s face turned deathly pale. “At least twenty people!” he repeated inwardly.

“Let me tell you something,” he continued, “Wèi-qiánbèi is absolutely handsome—not scary at all.”

“That’s right!” another disciple confirmed. “Worried that we might misunderstand HánGuāng-Jūn’s behaviour, Wèi-qiánbèi told us that the members of the GūsūLán Clan aren’t required to restrain themselves when they’re with their beloved partners.”

“That’s true only when you’re alone with your partner, not in public,” Lán Qǐrén muttered to himself, already on the verge of collapse.

Another boy said, “Since Wèi-qiánbèi was feeling unwell, it was very considerate of HánGuāng-Jūn to carry him the whole time. They really are a perfect match—it’s so enviable.”

“Come to think of it,” one of them said, “a disciple of the GūsūLán told us later that HánGuāng-Jūn went to the kitchen while still holding Wèi-qiánbèi in his arms.”

At that, Lán Qǐrén couldn’t keep himself upright any longer. He stumbled at the threshold and fell inside the classroom.

<b>Extra 5:

An Argument Between Jiāng Chéng And Jīn Líng</b>

Furious, Jīn Líng asked in a loud voice, “Which of you dares to bully my uncle? Step forward!”

The branch leaders were left speechless. The fact that there were seven of them ganging up on one person made it clear that they had been bullying him, and it was difficult for them to deny the accusation.

Suddenly, Jiāng Chéng pulled Jīn Líng into his arms without saying a word.

Jīn Líng felt awkward. Although he was moved, there was a time and place for everything. He couldn’t help nudging his uncle.

He said, “Ahem... Uncle, let go of me. You’re acting just like Wèi WúXiàn. It’s hardly proper behaviour.”

At that moment, a chill ran down everyone’s spines—Yílíng Lǎozǔ was nearby! They were doomed to be blamed.

Jiāng Chéng slowly let go of Jīn Líng and, in a hoarse voice, asked, “Where have you been?”

“I went to catch Jīn Chǎn,” Jīn Líng replied.

Jiāng Chéng snapped, “And instead, you got caught and had to be rescued by Wèi WúXiàn! What were you trying to prove? Acting like a hero in the dead of night when you’re just a kid!”

“Wèi WúXiàn just happened to be there,” Jīn Líng replied. “I can handle things on my own, so can you please stop treating me like a kid all the time?”

Jiāng Chéng retorted, “Don’t try to put on a brave face. Just look at yourself—you’re covered in dust and look nothing like a sect leader. Do you really think that I don’t know what you’re made of?”

“Yes, you just don’t know!” Jīn Líng exclaimed.

He then added, “Besides, it’s you, Uncle, who has been bullied into a state of utter terror. Does looking extremely dejected and dispirited befit a sect leader?”

Jiāng Chéng completely lost his cool. “What do you think caused me to be in such a dispirited state? And who exactly are you saying has been bullied?” he thundered.

The branch leaders seemed to think, “It’s us who have been bullied by him. Thank you, Jiāng Zōng-zhǔ, for pointing it out.”

“Wow!” Wèi WúXiàn exclaimed. “Do you really have to go through all those documents?”

Jīn Líng raised an eyebrow and asked, “What are you doing here?”

“Just passing by,” Wèi WúXiàn replied.

Jīn Líng recalled what Jiāng Chéng had said earlier and realised that Wèi WúXiàn must have come there to help him.

Trying to suppress his emotions, Jīn Líng said, “You really have too much free time.”

Wèi WúXiàn only smiled. He stepped forward and began to sort through the documents that were piled like mountains on Jīn Líng’s desk.

Jīn Líng stared at Wèi WúXiàn’s slender silhouette, wanting to say something, but he held back.

Wèi WúXiàn noticed his hesitation and immediately asked, “Do you have something on your mind?”

After a brief hesitation, Jīn Líng began, “You and Uncle...”

“What about him?” Wèi WúXiàn asked.

Jīn Líng wanted to know whether Jiāng Chéng had caused him any trouble, but instead he asked, “Did you two get into a fight?”

Wèi WúXiàn blinked, then a smile slowly appeared on his face. He asked, “Are you concerned about me?”

Jīn Líng quickly denied it. “Not at all! I’m worried about my uncle.”

Wèi WúXiàn smiled brightly and said, “You’re worried about him? Don’t you mean you’re worried that he can’t beat me?”

“What!” Jīn Líng exclaimed. “How could I possibly worry about that?”

“Oh!” Wèi WúXiàn said. “I had no idea that you thought so highly of me.”

Jīn Líng said, “Stop being so full of yourself!”

“All right, then,” Wèi WúXiàn said. “Since neither of those is the case, I will just assume that you care about me.”

As soon as he finished speaking, Wèi WúXiàn affectionately ruffled Jīn Líng’s hair. He then chuckled and added, “Even if it’s not me that you’re worried about, it still makes me happy. Thank you.”

Jīn Líng’s face turned beetroot red. He was completely lost for words.

Jīn Líng felt embarrassed at the thought that Wèi WúXiàn had heard him scream. He began, “I was just...”

“Just what?” Wèi WúXiàn asked. “Did you think it would be more embarrassing to hide behind the donkey or to use it to escape?”

Jīn Líng’s expression darkened as he pictured himself making his escape on the donkey.

Wèi WúXiàn slapped him on the head, scolding, “When you are about to die, what’s the point of saving face? Is this what your uncle taught you?”

“Don’t speak ill of my uncle!” Jīn Líng retorted.

Rubbing the sore spot on his head, he added, “Besides, my spiritual veins had been sealed, so there was no way I could escape. Those people were probably out to capture me alive.”

“No matter what, from now on, if you can’t win a fight, just run away,” Wèi WúXiàn said. “Don’t try to be a hero.”

As he realised that Jīn Líng hadn’t taken him seriously, Wèi WúXiàn placed his hands on Jīn Líng’s shoulders and held them firmly.

He continued sternly, “Jīn Líng, you must never jump from such a height again. Do you hear me?”

If he hadn’t immediately sent numerous spirits to search for Jīn Líng as soon as he reached the Lánlíng territory, Wèi WúXiàn wouldn’t have arrived in time to save him.

His heart had skipped a beat as he rushed over and saw Jīn Líng falling from the sky. Even now, he couldn’t shake the fear he had felt.

Looking at Wèi WúXiàn’s solemn expression, Jīn Líng finally nodded, then couldn’t help asking, “How did you find me?”

Xiǎo-Píngguǒ* brayed in reply, and Jīn Líng felt mortified.

<small> *Xiǎo-Píngguǒ is the name of Wèi WúXiàn’s donkey. Literally, it means little apple.</small>

Wèi WúXiàn smiled as he handed the sword back to Jīn Líng, saying, “Xiǎo-Píngguǒ was not the only one tracking your whereabouts.”

He then asked, “By the way, what are you planning to do with those four scoundrels?”

Jīn Líng glanced at them and replied, “I’ll take them back and make them spill the beans about Jīn Chǎn’s hideout.”

Wèi WúXiàn chuckled and said, “That’s easy enough. Let me handle it.”

As soon as he finished speaking, the living corpses crawled up the men’s bodies, seemingly eager to suck their blood and devour their flesh, making the already terrified men panic even more.

One of them couldn’t hold out any longer and cried, “We... we were sent by Jīn Mǐn. He only instructed us to kidnap Jīn Zōng-zhǔ. We don’t know where Jīn Chǎn is.”

“So it was him!” Jīn Líng exclaimed in surprise.

Wèi WúXiàn asked, “Who is Jīn Mǐn?”

“He’s Jīn Chǎn’s father,” Jīn Líng replied. “They think that once I’m out of the picture, everything will go smoothly for them.”

He then asked, “By the way, why are you on your own? Where’s HánGuāng-Jūn?”

“When I arrived here, I sent a spirit to track down Jīn Chǎn, and Lán Zhàn went with it. They’ll be back once they’ve found him,” Wèi WúXiàn replied.

“In other words, all we have to do is wait to catch our fish!” Jīn Líng said in astonishment. He had to admit that Demonic Cultivation was truly amazing.

Smiling, Wèi WúXiàn looked at Jīn Líng for a moment, then said, “While we wait, how about we practise a bit?”

Jīn Líng’s eye lit up. Whenever they went for Night Hunting, Wèi WúXiàn would always find some spare time for the youngsters to spar with one another.

Each time, he would teach them new moves tailored to the individual. His teaching was extremely useful. How could he pass up such a good opportunity?

When he was about to draw Suìhuá, Wèi WúXiàn stopped him, saying, “We’re not going to practise the sword techniques yet. You still have a long way to go with your footwork.”

Before Jīn Líng could protest, Wèi WúXiàn snapped his fingers, and the living corpses obeyed, dragging three of the four scoundrels aside and leaving one standing in the middle of the clearing.

Still shaking, the man watched Wèi WúXiàn walk towards him, then stop about fifteen metres away.

Wèi WúXiàn tucked Chénqíng into his waistband. Tilting his head to one side, he said, “Take that man, for example. His physical build is just perfect for you to practise your footwork.”

Jīn Líng examined the man with curiosity, then asked, “How can you tell?”

Wèi WúXiàn smiled, then replied, “He’s both taller and stronger than you. However, he lacks flexibility. Against someone like him, you can use this move.”

No sooner had he finished speaking than, like a flash, he darted forwards and instantly appeared next to the man.

Startled, the man panicked and hurriedly threw an uppercut. Wèi WúXiàn remained calm and casually sidestepped to dodge it. He swiftly formed a knife hand and struck the scoundrel in the neck.

The man staggered to the right as he took the blow. Wèi WúXiàn immediately raised his foot and pressed it down on the man’s right knee pit, causing him to fall to the ground. He then put him in an armlock, pinning him down.

Jīn Líng, who had been a little bit frustrated by Wèi WúXiàn’s remark, was now eager to give it a try. Wèi WúXiàn smiled at him.

He then placed a puppeting talisman on the man’s back and released his grip before stepping aside.

Controlled by Wèi WúXiàn, the man immediately charged at Jīn Líng in a fury.

Jīn Líng knew that Wèi WúXiàn was doing this for his training, so he braced himself. Recalling Wèi WúXiàn’s demonstration, he assumed the correct stance, readying himself to face his opponent head-on.

Standing aside, Wèi WúXiàn watched the fight with great interest. From time to time, he would clap his hands to make his puppet pause instantly, step forward to correct Jīn Líng’s fighting posture, then step back and snap his fingers, ordering the puppet to resume the fight.

After sparring with the four men, Jīn Líng, drenched in sweat, was panting hard. Yet his eyes were sparkling with sheer excitement, revealing a desire for more.

Wèi WúXiàn smiled with satisfaction and said, “Not bad at all. If you practise those moves with your uncle, you’ll become quite proficient.”

“What?!” Jīn Líng cried. “You want me to practise with my uncle!”

Stunned, Wèi WúXiàn exclaimed, “Don’t tell me you’ve never sparred with him, not even once!”

“I have... only once,” Jīn Líng replied shyly. “He completely outclassed me. Ever since then, he hasn’t sparred with me at all. I only practise with his disciples.”

Wèi WúXiàn was speechless, wondering which of Jiāng Chéng’s disciples was brave enough to spar with Jīn Líng without holding back. No wonder Jīn Líng’s skills were lacking.

Frustrated, he said, “Just keep asking him to spar with you. He’s stronger than you, but he won’t hurt you. Where else can you find such a good sparring partner?”

He continued, “Let me tell you something. Jiāng Chéng is soft-hearted but sharp-tongued. As long as you keep flattering him, saying things like, ‘Uncle, you’re so amazing! I really admire you. Can you teach me a few moves?’ you’ll see that he won’t be able to refuse you.”

Jīn Líng got goosebumps picturing himself saying all those things. He was about to reply sharply when he saw Lán WàngJī emerge from the woods.

Lán WàngJī was undeniably handsome, like a celestial being; his elegance was unparalleled. He seemed to glow in the moonlight as he walked towards them.

Wèi WúXiàn turned around and smiled at him, then asked, “Lán Zhàn, have you found where Jīn Chǎn has been hiding?”

Lán WàngJī hesitated for a moment before replying, “I have.”

Overjoyed, Jīn Líng asked, “Where is it?”

Lán WàngJī seemed hesitant to speak again.

While Wèi WúXiàn looked at him thoughtfully, Jīn Líng said earnestly, “HánGuāng-Jūn, please tell me where he is. Come what may – be it a den of dragons or a lair of tigers – I must catch him. Otherwise, I won’t be able to explain myself in tomorrow’s meeting.”

Lán WàngJī replied, “...He’s in NingXiang Pavilion*.”

<small>*NingXiang Pavilion means a pavilion that brings together strong and lasting aromas.</small>

Wèi WúXiàn chuckled. “What a lovely name,” he remarked. “Since you’re in such a hurry, let’s get straight away then.”

Jīn Líng’s face flushed. “No way!” he cried.

Laughing, Wèi WúXiàn said, “What’s the matter? Didn’t you just say you wanted to capture him even if it meant going into a dragon’s den or a tiger’s lair?”

Jīn Líng grew so flustered that he couldn’t help but stutter, “But that, that place is... is...”

“...a brothel.” Wèi WúXiàn finished the sentence. “Rumours about Jīn Chǎn being lecherous by nature turn out to be true. No wonder none of you could find him.”

Ever since Jīn GuāngYáo’s wrongdoings came to light, the LánlíngJīn Sect had suffered a severe blow to its reputation.

To restore their standing, they had established a new rule that forbade all members from frequenting brothels or indulging in sensual pleasures.

Because of that new rule, the YúnmèngJiāng’s disciples assigned to find Jīn Chǎn’s whereabouts never thought of looking for him in the brothels.

Jīn Líng thought for a moment, then glanced at Wèi WúXiàn uncertainly.

Wèi WúXiàn hurriedly said, “Don’t look at me like that. I’m a married man now, so I can’t enter that kind of place.”

Lán WàngJī glanced at Wèi WúXiàn; his expression revealed neither pleasure nor displeasure.

“What should we do then?” Jīn Líng asked.

With a mischievous expression, Wèi WúXiàn replied, “Those who accomplish great things don’t concern themselves with trivialities.”

He then added, “Don’t worry. As long as no one finds out you’re the leader of the LánlíngJīn Sect, you can leave the rest to me.”

Without further ado, the three of them set off.

Upon arriving at their destination, Wèi WúXiàn frowned as he was assailed by the strong smell of rouge and face powder. Accustomed to Lán WàngJī’s sandalwood scent, he found the heady fragrance of the cosmetics almost suffocating.

He suddenly regretted letting Lán WàngJī come along. Moving closer, he affectionately traced a finger across Lán WàngJī’s palm.

“Lán Zhàn, can you wait here for me? he said. “I’m going in with Jīn Líng. I’ll be right back.”

Lán WàngJī nodded. Jīn Líng entrusted his sword to him to avoid arousing suspicion.

Wèi WúXiàn placed a talisman on Jīn Líng’s back and watched with satisfaction as it transformed Jīn Líng’s features.

Despite being familiar with Wèi WúXiàn’s talismans, this was the first time Jīn Líng had seen that kind of talisman.

Wèi WúXiàn put the spirit that had helped them locate Jīn Chǎn inside Jīn Líng’s bag. Then he casually led Jīn Líng, and the two of them entered NingXiang Pavilion.

Jīn Líng nervously twisted his sleeves. His body stiffened as he watched several young women approach them one after another, their eyes lighting up.

When he noticed that the girls were dressed enticingly, Jīn Líng didn’t know where to look. He forced himself to remain calm and glanced at Wèi WúXiàn.

Smiling casually, Wèi WúXiàn looked perfectly at ease. Every feature of his face exuded an indescribable charm and grace. With his good looks and extraordinary presence, he caught everyone’s eye, while the corner of Jīn Líng’s mouth twitched.

The madam could easily tell that those two men were no ordinary people. She walked over and politely greeted them.

“It’s rare to see two such distinguished gentlemen like yourselves. May I ask what kind of girls you would like to keep you company?” she asked.

Surrounded by young women, Wèi WúXiàn led Jīn Líng to a table in the centre of the room. As he sat down, a girl quickly poured him a cup of wine.

Wèi WúXiàn leaned back slightly, accepted the cup the girl offered him and replied, “That’s really a difficult question, since all the ladies here are so beautiful. Any of them would do.”

He added, “I wonder which of these ladies would be kind enough to join me for a drink?”

With a casual flick of his slender fingers, Wèi WúXiàn tossed a silver ingot into the madam’s hands.

When she noticed that it was pure silver, the madam was delighted and said, “Girls, make sure to properly entertain this gentleman.”

She then looked at Jīn Líng and asked, “I’d like to know what sort of entertainment you prefer, young gentleman.”

Jīn Líng was about to say he didn’t need anything at all when Wèi WúXiàn, gracefully holding his cup of wine and smiling at him, suddenly said, “My young brother here is a little bit tired. May I trouble that young lady in red to take him upstairs so that he could rest?”

Jīn Líng felt as though he had been struck by lightning.

Wèi WúXiàn leisurely sipped his wine, then winked at Jīn Líng.

With a smile, the girl in red walked over and hooked her arm around Jīn Líng’s, causing him to tense up instantly.

Glancing at her, Jīn Líng noticed that, although the girl’s expression seemed normal, her gaze was vacant. He then caught sight of a puppeting talisman stuck on her back.

Surprised, Jīn Líng wondered when exactly Wèi WúXiàn had placed it there. The talisman was well hidden behind the girl’s long black hair. Relieved, he relaxed and let her lead him upstairs.

When they reached the upper floor, they walked past several doors – some open, some closed – until they arrived in front of a red-lacquered, carved wooden door.

At that moment, Jīn Líng’s bag suddenly trembled. A burst of ghostly energy surged out, seeped through the crack in the door, unlocked it and pulled it open.

The bang startled the person inside the room. Then, behind the gauze curtains of the bed canopy, he shouted irritably, “Who dares to be so insolent?”

Jīn Líng sneered, cracked his knuckles and walked into the room. He kicked the table before replying, “You should take a good look at who is being insolent yourself.”

Downstairs, people were puzzled by a sudden commotion, followed by a brawl.

Worried, the madam quickly ordered her enforcers to go upstairs and see what was going on.

She then bowed to Wèi WúXiàn and said, “I’m terribly sorry. It seems that someone is causing trouble and disturbing your enjoyment. Allow me to go there and handle it myself.”

Wèi WúXiàn watched the madam’s men rushing upstairs and replied, “Certainly.”

With a smirk playing on his lips, he murmured to himself, “I’m quite curious to see how long Jīn Líng would take to throw those men out of the room.”

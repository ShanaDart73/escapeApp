<br />

After staring at the three items for a while, Wèi WúXiàn began, “Lán Zhàn, take Yīn-Hǔfú—” He interrupted himself when he looked up at Lán WàngJī, his eyes widening as he saw himself wear the GūsūLán uniform.

Puzzled, Lán WàngJī asked, “What about Yīn-Hǔfú?”

“Ah? Oh...” Wèi WúXiàn composed himself. “You should keep Yīn-Hǔfú because there are quite a lot of people out there who want me dead. With Yīn-Hǔfú in your possession, no one will dare cause you trouble.”

Lán WàngJī shook his head and said, “There’s no need.”

Wèi WúXiàn tried to persuade him, saying, “But my body can’t even wield a sword, and you don’t know how to use demonic energy to defend yourself.”

In a resolute tone, Lán WàngJī said, “Don’t worry. As long as I’m here, no one can lay a finger on you.”

Wèi WúXiàn blinked, wondering whether he had heard correctly. He asked, “What did you just say?”

Realising what he had said, Lán WàngJī was stunned as well. He suddenly turned around as though he wanted to conceal something from Wèi WúXiàn, then replied, “I’m at Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù, and no one here will attack me.”

“Oh,” Wèi WúXiàn murmured.

By the time he wanted to say something else, Wèi WúXiàn realised that Lán WàngJī had already left the room.

He then wondered, “Was I imagining things? Did Lán Zhàn seem to... blush just now? Did I say something that made him feel embarrassed?”

Recalling the earlier sight of himself dressed in the GūsūLán uniform, the corners of Wèi WúXiàn’s mouth curved into a smile.

“Surprisingly, the mourning-like* attire of the GūsūLán Sect looks quite good on me,” Wèi WúXiàn muttered.

<small>*In the traditional Chinese culture, people wear white for the funerals.</small>

“What’s with everyone calling me Yílíng Lǎozǔ? It makes me sound like a fearsome old man.

“I, Wèi WúXiàn, rank fourth among the younger generation of the noble families, after all.

“If I get the chance, I’ll definitely wear the GūsūLán attire and show off in front of those girls in Yúnmèng. Who knows, they might even give me a few trinkets or treats.”

At that moment, his gaze fell upon the lotus flower engraved on the hilt of his sword, and his smile instantly froze.

“Have you forgotten?” a voice echoed in his head. “You have already defected from the YúnmèngJiāng Sect. You even caused the deaths of Jiāng YànLí and her husband.”

“You are no longer a member of the YúnmèngJiāng Sect. You are now Yílíng Lǎozǔ, reviled by all. How could you possibly have a chance to return? How could you even have the nerve to go back?”

Wèi WúXiàn’s gaze slowly drifted to the jet-black Yīn-Hǔfú. He had planned to find a way to destroy it when he discovered how harmful it was.

Before doing so, he needed some time to recover from both his injuries and his grief. However, unexpectedly, he ended up swapping souls with Lán WàngJī.

“In this badly wounded body I’m occupying,” Wèi WúXiàn thought, “setting aside how long it will be before I’m able to get out of bed, let alone whether I’ll manage to walk, if I have to destroy Yīn-Hǔfú now, I’ll deprive Lán Zhàn of the very thing that can protect him, leaving him completely vulnerable.”

<br />&nbsp;

When he stepped outside Jingshi, Lán WàngJī saw Lán XīChén waiting for him by the door. He then realised that his elder brother had been standing there ever since he had left the room.

Upon seeing his younger brother’s new appearance, Lán XīChén was stunned. He then smiled and said, “You’re ready.”

Lán WàngJī nodded and said, “Xiōng-zhǎng, sorry to have kept you waiting.”

Lán XīChén replied, “Never mind. Let’s go.”

Instead of taking the main path, Lán XīChén led his younger brother along a small, less-frequented path.

Fully aware of the reason behind his elder brother’s actions, Lán WàngJī said nothing and simply followed.

“I haven’t told anyone about you swapping souls with Wèi-gōngzǐ,” Lán XīChén said after they had walked a short distance. “When you arrived earlier, most of the disciples were attending classes, so we didn’t encounter anyone along the way.”

“However, it’s almost the end of the lessons now, and I’m worried that if they see Yílíng Lǎozǔ’s face here in Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù, it will cause a commotion. That’s why we’ve taken this secluded path. You should bear that in mind whenever you want to move about.”

Lán WàngJī replied, “Thank you for your concern, Xiōng-zhǎng. I’ll beat it in mind.”

As they passed a dwelling, Lán XīChén stopped and said, “The Wen clansmen are staying in this guest house. Would you like to check in on them?”

As he nodded, Lán WàngJī replied, “Yes.” Then he followed Lán XīChén, and both entered the guest house.

The Wen clansmen had just finished eating and were sitting quietly, occasionally glancing towards the door as though they were expecting someone.

Although Lán XīChén had told them not to stand on ceremony and to treat the place as if it were their own home, they still felt a little tense despite the warm hospitality they had received.

Besides, knowing very well how much importance the GūsūLán Sect placed on propriety, how could they behave otherwise?

As soon as they saw Lán XīChén and “Wèi WúXiàn” walk in, the Wen clansmen hurriedly rose to their feet and greeted them, “Lán Zōng-zhǔ and Wèi-gōngzǐ.”

After returning the greeting, Lán XīChén asked in a gentle tone, “Was the food to your liking?”

Sì-Shū hurriedly replied, “It was to our liking. Thank you, Lán Zōng-zhǔ.”

Then, scratching his head, he awkwardly added, “We’re sorry to have caused you so much trouble.”

“It’s no trouble at all,” Lán XīChén replied. “You all came along with Wèi-gōngzǐ, and since we have welcomed him into our residence, it’s only natural for us to accommodate you as well.”

At that moment, he suddenly felt something weighing on his left leg. Looking down, he saw a little boy of about two years old wrapping his arms and legs around his leg.

“Yǒuqián-gēge*,” the child called out, grinning up at him.

<small>*Yǒuqián-gēge means rich brother</small>

Lán XīChén’s eyes widened in surprise as he asked, “Who is this child?”

“It’s Wēn Yuàn, our child,” a voice replied. “Ā-Yuàn, come over here.”

It was Wēn Yuàn’s grandma who had spoken. She then stepped forward and picked him up before saying apologetically, “Ā-Yuàn mistook you for your younger brother. Please forgive him for offending you, Lán Zōng-zhǔ.”

Smiling, Lán XīChén shook his head and said, “He’s a lovely child.”

Wēn Yuàn then noticed “Wèi WúXiàn” and called out, “Xiàn-gēge.”

However, the next instant, he felt that something wasn’t quite right. Coming to a realisation, he muttered, “Huh? Xiàn-gēge is wearing white clothes?”

Having overheard Wēn Yuàn’s remark, Lán XīChén couldn’t suppress a chuckle. He turned towards Lán WàngJī and said, “He’s calling you. Aren’t you going to hug him?”

After a brief hesitation, Lán WàngJī stepped forward and took the child from his grandma’s arms.

Wēn Yuàn immediately buried his head against Lán WàngJī’s chest and asked in a low, muffled voice, “Xiàn-gēge, do you still feel pain?”

Shocked, Lán WàngJī’s body stiffened. “What?” he said.

“Xiàn-gēge, I saw you bleeding a lot. You were also in pain. Grandma stopped when I wanted to get closer to you,” the child replied. “I also heard you crying. Xiàn-gēge, let me give you a kiss so the pain will go away.”

Putting his words into action, Wēn Yuàn raised his head and kissed Lán WàngJī on the cheek.

“Wèi-gōngzǐ, Ā-Yuàn—” Sì-Shū, who had intended to take the child away, was stopped by Lán WàngJī.

“It’s all right,” he replied, but the tremor in his voice betrayed the sorrow he felt.

Wen Yuàn was oblivious to what he had just revealed. When his gaze fell on Lán XīChén, he said to him, “Yǒuqián-gēge, Xiàn-gēge has lost a lot of blood. Can you check on him?”

Before Lán XīChén could reply, Lán WàngJī said, “Ā-Yuàn, Xiàn-gēge will no longer bleed or be in pain.”

He then ruffled the child’s hair and firmly added, “It won’t happen again.”

After a few more words of reassurance, Lán WàngJī handed the child to his grandma before addressing them all, “Stay here for now and don’t leave your quarters unless it’s absolutely necessary.

“If you ever need anything, take the small path and head east. That path will lead you to Jingshi. You’ll find me and HánGuāng-Jūn there at any time.”

The Wen clansmen naturally expressed their gratitude to both of them.

After taking their leave, Lán XīChén and Lán WàngJī continued walking towards Lán Qǐrén’s quarters.

Then, suddenly, Lán XīChén remarked, “Earlier, when you all arrived, I didn’t notice that there was a lively child among them.”

“He must have fallen asleep at the time,” Lán WàngJī replied.

“The child seemed to have mistaken me for you. Have you two met before?” Lán XīChén asked.

“Yes,” he replied. “I happened to run into Wèi Yīng when I went to Yílíng for Night Hunting. That day, Wèi Yīng was in town with Ā-Yuàn.”

Lán XīChén immediately saw the full picture.

Despite Lán XīChén’s attempt to cheer up his younger brother, Lán WàngJī still wore a sorrowful expression.

Sighing, Lán XīChén said, “WàngJī, you can no longer change what has already happened. Besides, you’re not responsible for the injuries Wèi-gōngzǐ has sustained. Why are you tormenting yourself like this?”

In a low voice, Lán WàngJī replied, “I know.”

Yet, whenever he thought about Wèi WúXiàn lying covered in blood and crying out in pain, alone in Fúmó Cave, Lán WàngJī felt his heart tighten. Why did someone so radiant have to endure such profound suffering? Above all, Wèi WúXiàn was the person he cherished most.

The two brothers walked in complete silence.

As they neared their destination, Lán XīChén asked, “Have you thought about how you’re going to explain the matter to Uncle?”

“I’ll tell him the truth,” Lán WàngJī replied.

Lán XīChén was stunned. Then, smiling, he said, “Indeed, there’s no need to hide the truth.”

As they reached Lán Qǐrén’s dwelling, Lán XīChén called out, “Uncle, are you at home?”

Lán Qǐrén replied, “Yes, I am. Come in.”

Lán XīChén pushed open the door and stepped in, followed by Lán WàngJī.

As he had just finished marking the disciples’ notes, Lán Qǐrén put down his brush and looked up. His eyes suddenly widened as he saw “Wèi WúXiàn” standing next to Lán XīChén.

He began, “You...”

Lán WàngJī stepped forward, knelt down and saluted, “Uncle, it’s me, WàngJī.”

Lán Qǐrén frowned, sensing that something was amiss. He asked, “What’s going on?”

Lán XīChén replied, “Uncle, for some unknown reason, WàngJī and Wèi-gōngzǐ have had their souls swapped. WàngJī is now occupying Wèi-gōngzǐ’s body.”

Lán Qǐrén meticulously examined Lán WàngJī, who was still kneeling in front of him. Apart from his face, the way he had performed the salute, his perfect posture and the way he tied his forehead ribbon all indicated that he was indeed his nephew.

He then said, “Stand up.”

Lán WàngJī did as he was told but kept his head bowed, avoiding his uncle’s gaze.

Lán Qǐrén quietly studied him for a while. Apparently, he found Wèi WúXiàn’s face truly infuriating. Frowning even more deeply, he asked, “Where is Wèi Yīng at this moment?”

Lán WàngJī replied, “In Jingshi.”

After a moment’s thought, Lán Qǐrén asked, “Did you come here from Yílíng?”

Lán WàngJī replied, “Yes.”

“Now that you’ve come to Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù, what has become of the Wen clansmen?” Lán Qǐrén asked.

Lán WàngJī knelt down once more before replying, “I took the liberty of bringing them along with me without permission.”

Lán Qǐrén remained silent. There was no sign of anger on his face—on the contrary, his expression seemed more relaxed, as though he were satisfied with his nephew’s decision.

Lán XīChén noticed his uncle’s expression and took it as a sign of approval. He said, “Uncle, those Wen clansmen are all innocent. If we leave them to fend for themselves, they will run into trouble. How about letting them live here in Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù?”

Wèi WúXiàn lay sprawled on the bed, his mind somewhat clouded by pain. He had a vague feeling that he had overlooked something important, but he just couldn’t remember what it was.

His gaze wandered aimlessly around the room before falling upon Bìchén* hanging in one corner. His heart gave a sudden jolt, and his pupils contracted sharply.

<small>*Bìchén is the name of Lán WàngJī’s sword.</small>

He thought, “Oh no! The Golden Core!”

Wèi WúXiàn finally realised why Lán WàngJī had not returned, even after so many days had passed.

“There is no Golden Core in my body, so Lán Zhàn can’t ride the sword back to Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù,” he thought.

As for the demon generals under his command, they were bound to him, and Lán WàngJī didn’t understand the method he used, so there was no way Lán WàngJī could command them.

Without Bìchén and WàngJī*, it was uncertain whether Lán WàngJī could subdue the evil creatures in the mountains, not to mention dealing with any unexpected incidents that might arise along the way.

<small>*WàngJī is the name of Lán WàngJī’s guqin.</small>

As things stood, returning to Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù wasn’t an easy task for Lán WàngJī.

“It’s so frustrating!” Wèi WúXiàn thought, somewhat exasperated. “Why did it only occur to me now?”

Although he had harboured a slightly mischievous impulse that made him reluctant to get involved, he had never actually intended to cause Lán WàngJī any serious trouble.

Wèi WúXiàn immediately shifted slightly, causing Lán XīChén, who was changing his bandage, to pause and ask, “Is something wrong?”

Then he saw his “younger brother” make a gesture—which was out of character—and wondered if he was seeing things.

“Lán WàngJī” beckoned him closer before saying in a hoarse voice, “Zéwú-Jūn, move a little closer, because I’m having trouble speaking aloud.”

Looking rather bewildered, Lán XīChén moved closer and asked, “WàngJī, how... did you just address me?”

Wèi WúXiàn replied, “I’m not Lán Zhàn.”

Lán XīChén was taken aback, wondering why his “younger brother” had said that.

Wèi WúXiàn said, “Uh, I’m not quite sure how to explain it. Anyway, I’m not Lán Zhàn—I’m Wèi Yīng.”

Upon hearing the name ‘Wèi Yīng’, Lán XīChén frowned. “WàngJī, have you lost your mind?” he said. “I know you care deeply for Wèi-gōngzǐ, but he has already brought a great calamity upon himself. You cannot atone for his sins, just as he cannot bear your punishment in your stead.”

Feeling helpless, Wèi WúXiàn scratched his head. He truly had no idea how to explain the bizarre situation he found himself in.

He repeated, “I’m really Wèi Yīng.”

After a short pause, he continued, “I don’t know what caused my soul to inhabit Lán Zhàn’s body. I simply woke up and found myself here.”

As he began to notice that his younger brother neither wore that kind of expression on his face nor spoke in his usual manner, Lán XīChén gradually became convinced.

His eyes suddenly widened as he asked, “What of WàngJī, then?”

“He’s probably occupying my body,” Wèi WúXiàn replied.

Lán XīChén asked, “How long have you been aware of this situation?”

“Today marks the seventh day,” he replied.

Upon hearing this, Lán XīChén shook his head and said, “WàngJī certainly doesn’t occupy your body. Otherwise, he would have returned to Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù by now.”

“Zéwú-Jūn, this is exactly what I want to discuss with you.”

Wèi WúXiàn didn’t want to dwell on why Lán XīChén was so certain that Lán WàngJī would rush back to Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù.

Turning his mind to more pressing matters, he said, “That body of mine can no longer ride upon a sword. If Lán Zhàn wants to return to GūsūLán, I’m afraid he’ll have to walk, and it will indeed take him quite a long time to get here.”

Lán XīChén could hardly hide his shocked expression. “Unable to fly on a sword?!” he exclaimed.

“That’s right,” Wèi WúXiàn replied.

Since he didn’t intend to go into details about his Golden Core, Wèi WúXiàn sidestepped Lán XīChén’s questioning gaze before continuing, “Well... considering what happened in Nightless Sky City recently, many people in the cultivation world will definitely recognise the face of Yílíng Lǎozǔ. I’m worried that Lán Zhàn might run into trouble on his way here.”

Lán XīChén glanced at him meaningfully as he thought, “Unexpectedly, Wèi-gōngzǐ is actually concerned about WàngJī’s safety.”

He then asked, “If that is the case, why didn’t you say so sooner?”

Wèi WúXiàn gave a slight, awkward smile before replying, “That’s because I had forgotten about it until now.”

“Zéwú-Jūn, you should send someone to search for Lán Zhàn as soon as possible,” he urged. “It’s already been seven days!”

“I’ll dispatch a few people to look for him,” Lán XīChén replied, yet he didn’t rise immediately but continued gazing at Wèi WúXiàn with a complicated expression.

“Zéwú-Jūn, is there something else you want to tell me?” Wèi WúXiàn asked.

Lán XīChén’s lips stirred, as if he were about to say something.

At that precise moment, a disciple’s voice rang out from outside Jingshi, reporting, “My Lord, Yílíng Lǎozǔ has arrived with a group of Wen clansmen. He is at the mountain gate, requesting an audience with you.”

The two of them exchanged glances, then Lán XīChén replied, “All right. I’m going there to receive them personally. Don’t let anyone know about this.”

“Understood,” the disciple replied before leaving.

Lán XīChén stood up and, before walking towards the door, glanced at Wèi WúXiàn once more. Then he sighed and murmured to himself, “Never mind. It’s better to let WàngJī tell him himself.”

Lán WàngJī stood outside the mountain gate of Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù, with Wen clansmen behind him. They had travelled on foot all the way from the Burial Mounds to GūsūLán.

That day, after giving them their instructions, Lán WàngJī had intended to fly to GūsūLán on Suíbiàn. Yet no sooner had he leapt onto the sword and risen a few feet into the air than he tumbled back down.

“Does this sword only allow its master to control it?” That was the first thought that crossed Lán WàngJī’s mind.

“But if it had already bonded with its master, I wouldn’t be able to draw it from its sheath,” he pondered. “I’ve never heard of a sword that could be drawn but was impossible to control.”

On an impulse, Lán WàngJī sat cross-legged on the spot and tried to channel his spiritual energy, only to discover that the dantian was completely empty.

“So that’s how it is,” Lán WàngJī thought. “He has been practising the demonic cultivation because he no longer possesses a Golden Core.”

“He continues down that precarious path because he has no other choice.”

“What happened to his Golden Core, then? Was it dissolved by that person known as the Core-Melting Hand?”

“No wonder he was so upset that day at the relay station. And yet, all I did was blame him.”

“Although I had sensed that something was amiss with his spiritual energy, I failed to realise that it was because of his Golden Core.”

“If only I had known sooner, perhaps... perhaps...”

Lán WàngJī clenched his fists tightly, but the next instant, he loosened them in resignation—what difference would it have made if he had known earlier? The world wouldn’t change just because of him, and Wèi WúXiàn would still be subjected to all kinds of false accusations.

His eyes became slightly bloodshot with frustration.

“Wèi-gōngzǐ?” someone called out cautiously.

Lán WàngJī raised his head and saw that the Wen clansmen had gathered nearby at some point. They had noticed the strange look on his face and were filled with concern, yet they dared not disturb him.

Lán WàngJī composed himself, stood up, brushed the dust off his clothes and asked, “What’s the matter?”

“We’ve finished packing,” Sì-Shū replied, looking somewhat embarrassed. “We only have a few belongings, so it didn’t take us long to pack. I’m just worried that there might not be enough money for travel expenses.”

Lán WàngJī was stunned, then gradually became distressed.

Throughout his life, he had never encountered a situation in which money was in short supply, nor had he ever worried about not having enough money for daily necessities such as food, clothing and other essentials.

As a result, he couldn’t understand Sì-Shū’s concerns.

Forcing himself to remain calm, Lán WàngJī nodded and said, “It doesn’t matter. I’ll go with you to GūsūLán.”

As things stood, it was obvious that Lán WàngJī couldn’t fly on the sword, which was just as well because, by travelling together, he could provide them with protection.

As for travel expenses, they would deal with that problem when the time came. For now, they would take one step at a time.

With that mindset, they set off on their journey to GūsūLán.

Because the money they had on hand was barely enough to meet their needs, they couldn’t afford to stay at an inn.

Before night fell, Lán WàngJī would search for a spot in the wilderness—a patch of woodland or a clearing—where they could spend the night. He would also assign people to guard the area overnight.

Throughout their journey, Lán WàngJī rested for only a few hours each day.

A few days later, their money ran out, so Lán WàngJī selected a few strong young men to accompany him on a hunt for wild game.

The bowstrings they had salvaged from a few broken bows along the way came in handy for hunting.

From time to time, they would come across cultivators who recognised Yílíng Lǎozǔ. However, before they could alert their fellow cultivators, their lips had already been sealed, and they couldn’t utter a single word.

By the time they recovered, Lán WàngJī and his companions had long since vanished without a trace.

Amid all the misfortunes, it was a stroke of luck that Lán WàngJī could still use the Silent Spell. And so, relying on his spell and a few bowstrings, Lán WàngJī managed to reach Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù.

Standing at the mountain gate, Lán WàngJī saw a white figure in the distance coming down the stone stairway. He stepped forward and, after a brief hesitation, bowed, saying, “Zéwú-Jūn.”

With a hint of helplessness in his voice, Lán XīChén said his name. “WàngJī.”

Surprised, Lán WàngJī raised his head and looked Lán XīChén in the eyes, then realised that his elder brother was already aware of the bizarre situation.

Lán WàngJī stepped slightly aside, bringing the Wen clansmen into Lán XīChén’s view, and bowed deeply once more. “Xiōng-zhǎng, I’m sorry for troubling you.”

Lán XīChén’s gaze swept over the faces of those gathered there, all of them anxious. He then said, “All of you, follow me.”

Before leading the Wen clansmen inside Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù, Lán XīChén said to Lán WàngJī, “Why don’t you go to Jingshi?”

Lán WàngJī remained silent for a short while, then said, “Thank you, Xiōng-zhǎng.”

The person he had long been in love with was, at that moment. in Jingshi, and the meaning behind Lán XīChén’s words was that Lán WàngJī should go and see him.

As he reached Jingshi, Lán WàngJī suddenly stopped at the doorway. He hadn’t forgotten how Wèi WúXiàn had told him to go away time and time again.

Back in the cave, when he asked Wèi WúXiàn why he had to do this or tried to comfort him, Wèi WúXiàn had told him to get lost.

When he finally mustered the courage to confess his feelings, Wèi WúXiàn had still told him to leave him alone.

Lán WàngJī was afraid that once he entered the room, Wèi WúXiàn would tell him to get lost again. However, he had to face it eventually.

He took a deep breath, then pushed open the door. As he walked in, Lán WàngJī saw his own body lying face down on the bed, his back covered in gruesome whip marks. However, the expression on his face was not his usual impassive one.

Wèi WúXiàn watched Lán WàngJī enter the room and looked slightly stunned. Then his lips curved into a smile. He winked at him before saying, “Lán Zhàn, long time no see.”

Lán WàngJī was in a daze after seeing such a dazzling smile. It was as though he had returned to the time when they had first met, three years ago.

Back then, he had been unable to resist Wèi WúXiàn’s teasing, while Wèi WúXiàn would laugh heartily whenever his little schemes succeeded.

Although it was a different face, Wèi WúXiàn’s smile had the same radiance as it had when it was on his own face.

It felt as though nothing had changed, yet everything had changed.

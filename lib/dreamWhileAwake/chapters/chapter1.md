Wèi WúXiàn’s sleep was cut short by an excruciating pain in his back. It felt as though his back had been slashed a thousand times with a knife. The pain was so intense that he struggled to breathe properly.

When he opened his eyes, he found himself lying face down on a bed in an unfamiliar bedroom, which was plain and simply furnished. The room was clean and tidy, yet it was filled with a strong smell of medicine.

Wèi WúXiàn didn’t realise that he was biting down on a folded handkerchief until he opened his mouth and it fell out.

He tried to pick it up but was struck by a sudden, sharp pain in his back. His whole body tensed up, and his eyes brimmed with tears.

He panted through the pain for a while before he could calm down.

“Have I been captured and subjected to torture by the righteous cultivators?” Wèi WúXiàn thought with a hint of irony.

“Never mind. Let them do as they please,” he continued. “Whether it’s death by a thousand cuts or having my bones crushed and my ashes scattered, I deserve every bit of it.”

Feeling drowsy, Wèi WúXiàn closed his eyes and drifted back to sleep.

He had a dreamless sleep—there was no shocked expression on Jīn ZiXuān’s* face, no bloodstained body of Jiāng YànLí*, no angry shouting from Jiāng Chéng, and no indiscriminate abuse from a myriad of cultivators.

<small>*Jīn ZiXuān and Jiāng YànLí are Jīn Líng’s parents.</small>

He felt only his heart aching as he stood all alone in a void. When he woke up, his eyes had welled up with tears, blurring his vision.

His body trembled uncontrollably, and his face streamed with moisture that had already soaked the pillow. He couldn’t tell whether it was sweat or tears.

“WàngJī, you’re awake.” The voice sounded quite familiar to Wèi WúXiàn.

“WàngJī?” Wèi WúXiàn thought. “Is he talking to Lán Zhàn? Then Lán Zhàn is here too.”

The same voice spoke again. “I’m going to change your bandage. It might hurt a bit, so just bear with it.”

“Changing the bandage?” Wèi WúXiàn wondered. “Is Lán Zhàn injured?”

“Ah! That’s right,” he continued. “It hasn’t been long since the incident in Nightless Sky City, so it’s no wonder that he’s injured. Maybe I was the one who wounded him.”

“What’s Lán Zhàn doing here?” Wèi WúXiàn pondered. “Oh! I suppose he’s in charge of keeping an eye on me, the demon lord.”

Then, as he hadn’t heard Lán WàngJī’s voice at all, Wèi WúXiàn became puzzled. But before he could think any further, his thoughts were suddenly interrupted when something was sprinkled onto his back, followed by a sharp, stinging pain that spread rapidly across it.

“Is he applying some medicine to my wounds?” Wèi WúXiàn thought in bewilderment.

Ignoring the pain in his back, he tried to raise his upper body but failed, falling back onto the bed.

In doing so, Wèi WúXiàn caught a glimpse of the person sitting beside him—it’s Lán XīChén.

Startled, Lán XīChén said, “WàngJī, what are you doing? Stay still.”

Lán XīChén put the medicine aside, took a handkerchief from his sleeve and then, leaning over “Lán WàngJī”, carefully wiped the sweat from his face.

Letting out a sigh, Lán XīChén said, “WàngJī, why do you have to torment yourself like this?”

Confused, Wèi WúXiàn turned his head slightly to look at Lán XīChén. Reflected in Lán XīChén’s eyes was his own face—or rather, Lán WàngJī’s face.

“Have I become Lán Zhàn?!” Wèi WúXiàn thought in disbelief. His eyes widened, and his face was filled with shock.

Lán XīChén looked at his younger brother’s unusual expression and felt that something wasn’t quite right.

He asked, “WàngJī, what’s wrong?”

Wèi WúXiàn wanted to say something but felt a burning pain in his throat and couldn’t make a sound at all.

“How did Lán Zhàn’s body I’m occupying get so badly injured?” he thought. “Given his level cultivation, even if he were attacked by a horde of fierce corpses, it wouldn’t have reduced him to such a state.”

Lán XīChén thought that his younger brother was at it again, worrying about Wèi WúXiàn. With a sad look on his face, he said, “Wèi-gōngzǐ has already caused a huge disaster, so why are you still being so stubborn?”

“What’s he being stubborn about?” Wèi WúXiàn wondered. Then he suddenly remembered, and a faint, wry smile appeared at the corner of his mouth.

He thought, “The little stickler still insists on taking me to GūsūLán to discipline me properly. He is indeed obstinate.”

“Come to think of it, I’m currently occupying his body. What about him? Could it be that he is occupying mine?”

He gave a scoffing sound deep in his throat. “I wonder what the pure and flawless HánGuāng-Jūn will think when he wakes up and finds himself transformed into the most despised and crooked Yílíng Lǎozǔ, whom he loathes above all else.”

“Will he kill my body with a single blow, acting out of righteous indignation to rid the people of the threat?”

With those thoughts in mind, Wèi WúXiàn found himself looking forward to it.

When Lán WàngJī woke up, he was in complete darkness and realised that not only was he no longer in Jingshi*, but he also no longer felt the agonising pain in his back caused by being slashed with a whip.

<small>*Jingshi is the name of Lán WàngJī’s dwelling.</small>

He sat up, chanted a spell and conjured a flame in his palm. Glancing around, he noticed that he was in a cave that seemed somewhat familiar.

A sudden thought crossed his mind, and he made his way towards a pool, guided by his recollection of the place.

He looked at his reflection on the surface of the water and saw the face of a fair-skinned youth. It was the person he thought about day and night, and the place had to be Fúmó Cave.

Unable to resist, Lán WàngJī slowly reached out to touch the reflected face. When his fingers came into contact with the icy-cold water, ripples formed, causing the image to blur.

Lán WàngJī remained in a daze for a while before withdrawing his fingers from the pool.

He had dreamt that a voice had told him it could help him fulfil his wish, but the price was that he would have to bear the full brunt of public condemnation.

He had not expected those words to mean that he would have to occupy Wèi WúXiàn’s body.

“Where is Wèi Yīng’s soul now?” Lán WàngJī wondered. “Could it be that his soul is in my body?”

He suddenly turned around and began to search the place. He then picked up a sword and walked towards the cave exit.

Two days ago, he had been punished with thirty-three slashes across his back with the dreadful whip, which was renowned for inflicting unbearable pain. How could he possibly let Wèi WúXiàn endure it in his place?

As soon as Lán WàngJī emerged from the cave, he saw the Wen clansmen gathered in front of the entrance, looking anxious. Upon seeing Lán WàngJī, or rather “Wèi WúXiàn”, they bowed to him in unison.

“Is there something you want to say?” Hearing Wèi WúXiàn’s voice coming out of his own mouth, Lán WàngJī was slightly stunned.

“Wèi-gōngzǐ,” one of them began, stepping forward. Lán WàngJī remembered Wèi WúXiàn calling him Sì-Shū.

Sì-Shū continued, “...don’t blame yourself for what happened to Miss Qíng and A-Níng.We were all saved by you. Every day we wake up and find ourselves still alive is a blessing. However, we’d rather be dead than drag you down with us.”

Lán WàngJī shook his head. Just like Wèi WúXiàn would rather bear the infamy than abandon those people, he, too, couldn’t bring himself to do so.

He didn’t know how Wèi WúXiàn would normally respond in that kind of situation, so he could only express his disapproval by remaining silent.

Sì-Shū urged, “Wèi-gōngzǐ, you should go and find a safe place for yourself. We don’t wish to burden you any longer.”

“What do you intend to do, then?” Lán WàngJī asked.

With a faint smile, Sì-Shū replied, “We... will wait quietly for our fate.”

Lán WàngJī glanced at them, one by one, and saw the same determination on their faces.

In a stern tone, he said, “I disapprove.”

They were all stunned by the unexpected harshness in his voice.

Lán WàngJī pondered for a moment, then said, “Try to gather some money for travel expenses, disguise yourselves and go to GūsūLán. Upon your arrival, ask for—”

He suddenly broke off, then continued, “Ask for Lán Zōng-zhǔ*.”

<small>*Zōng-zhǔ means sect leader.</small>

He had intended to say, ‘ask for HánGuāng-Jūn’, but as he was currently unable to help them, so he decided to let his elder brother handle the matter for the time being.

“I have something urgent to take care of and must set off at once,” he said. “We shall meet at Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù in seven days.”

After giving the Wen clansmen their instructions, Lán WàngJī tightened his grip on Suíbiàn* and headed down the mountain.

<small>*Suíbiàn is the name of Wèi WúXiàn’s sword.</small>

After taking a few steps, he stopped, turned around and said firmly, “Not a single person is allowed to stay behind.”

Wèi WúXiàn didn’t tell Lán XīChén that Lán WàngJī’s soul and his own had somehow swapped, leaving him to occupy Lán WàngJī’s body until the seventh day.

For one thing, Lán WàngJī’s body had been so severely injured that he spent most of the day asleep. For another, he had no interest in finding out anything about the soul-swapping matter at all.

His Shījiě* had passed away because of him, so Jiāng Chéng would never allow him to return to Lotus Piers. The Burial Mounds would probably be lost to him as well, leaving him with nowhere to return to.

<small>*Shījiě means senior sister. Wèi WúXiàn refers to Jīn Líng’s mother.</small>

So why should he care about regaining his own body when he had lost everything?

Besides, given Lán Èr-gōngzǐ’s sharp mind, once he woke up and realised that something was amiss with his body, it wouldn’t take him long to figure out what had happened.

Whether Lán WàngJī would kill the demon lord’s body with a single blow or return to Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù first was no concern of Wèi WúXiàn’s.

As a matter of fact, the GūsūLán Sect had always advocated the idea that moral cultivation came first, so Lán WàngJī would probably return there first thing.

What’s more, hadn’t Lán WàngJī wanted so badly to take him to GūsūLán and lock him up? Well, now the soul-swapping had done the work for him by bringing Wèi WúXiàn’s soul straight to Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù, and with Lán WàngJī’s body in such a sorry state, there was no way he could escape.

“What will become of the Wen clansmen in the Burial Mounds?” Wèi WúXiàn wondered.

Someone like HánGuāng-Jūn, who cared deeply for the people, wouldn’t indiscriminately label them as ‘dogs who deserved to die’. He would surely ensure that they were moved to a safe place before returning to Yúnshēn Bùzhīchù.

Arranging a new home for the Wen clansmen would probably take Lán WàngJī a day or two, and he would surely be back by the third day.

Days went by, and Wèi WúXiàn had someone attend to his wounds and bring him food and drink every day. Although the food wasn’t quite to his taste, he treated it as a blessing from HánGuāng-Jūn and enjoyed the few days he had left.

By the seventh day, however, there was still no news of Lán WàngJī’s return.
